Reading the gaps

An empty ✓ doesn't necessarily mean the provider exposes no content for that genre — it means the addon doesn't currently route that provider into the unified genre. Most gaps are deliberate: Blaze doesn't publish a catalogue API for filtering, so its unified-genre presence is just "all shows under every relevant unified genre"; Sky News is a single channel so the genre matrix doesn't apply.
