Usage

First launch

  1. Open Settings → Add-ons → EasyPlayTV → Configure
  2. Enable Do you have a TV license? to unlock BBC content
  3. Optionally enter your ITVX credentials (required for VOD), or Channel 4 / My5 credentials (for premium content only)
  4. If you installed the Service addon (Variant B), configure it under Settings → My add-ons → EasyPlayTV Service → Configure — PVR enabled, region, device role (master/follower)
  5. Restart Kodi — the Service addon / standalone service starts automatically

On first launch, the sync service will begin populating your library. This takes several hours for the full catalogue (6,000+ shows, 120,000+ episodes). Content appears progressively as each provider completes its first sync.

Playback-only installs (Variant A)

Without the Service addon or standalone service there is no sync and no PVR. You can still browse and play the live channel grid (and any VOD already in the shared library, if a master exists elsewhere).

Browsing content

Live TV

Open the addon from Kodi → Add-ons → EasyPlayTV → Live TV. You'll see a grid of channel icons. Radio channels appear in Kodi's Radio section. Click any channel to start playing.

Live TV via PVR (Variant B / C)

With a Service runtime installed, live channels also appear in Kodi's native TV and Radio sections with a full EPG guide. The Service addon configures pvr.iptvsimple automatically; the standalone service serves the channels.m3u + epg.xml over HTTP for you to point iptvsimple at.

Movies & TV Shows

After the first sync completes, movies and TV shows appear in Kodi's native Movies and TV Shows library windows — no need to browse through the addon's menu. They have full metadata: posters, fanart, plots, ratings, and genre tags.

Provider badges

All synced titles are prefixed with a provider badge: [ITVX], [C4], [My5], [STV], or [BLAZE]. This makes it easy to see which service each show comes from.

Account sign-in

ITVX

  1. Open Settings → Add-ons → EasyPlayTV → Configure → ITVX
  2. Enter your ITVX email and password
  3. Click Sign In

Sign-in is required for ITVX VOD content. ITVX live channels work without sign-in.

Channel 4

  1. Open Settings → Add-ons → EasyPlayTV → Configure → Channel 4
  2. Enter your Channel 4 email and password
  3. Click Sign In

Free-to-air Channel 4 content plays without sign-in. Sign-in is only needed for premium content.

My5

  1. Open Settings → Add-ons → EasyPlayTV → Configure → My5
  2. Enter your Channel 5 email and password
  3. Click Sign In

Free-to-air My5 content plays without sign-in. Sign-in is only needed for premium content that requires a My5 account.

Sync schedule

The Service runtime (Service addon master or standalone) syncs each provider on a staggered 6-hour schedule:

ProviderFirst syncRepeat
BBC iPlayer00:00Every 6 hours
ITVX00:30Every 6 hours
Channel 401:00Every 6 hours
My501:30Every 6 hours
STV Player02:00Every 6 hours
Blaze02:30Every 6 hours

The interval is configurable: VOD sync hours in the Service addon settings, or vod_sync.interval_hours in config.yaml for the standalone service.

Triggering a manual sync

Service addon (Variant B)

The Service addon polls for marker files every 30 seconds and fires the sync when it sees one. Write a marker file in the video addon's profile directory:

touch /home/osmc/.kodi/userdata/addon_data/plugin.video.easyplaytv/force_sync_c4

Available providers: bbc, itvx, c4, my5, stv, blaze, great.

Standalone service (Variant C)

Use the --sync flag to force a single provider sync, or --once-pvr to regenerate the PVR files:

PYTHONPATH=src ./.venv/bin/python -m easyplaytv_service.main --config config.yaml --sync my5
PYTHONPATH=src ./.venv/bin/python -m easyplaytv_service.main --config config.yaml --once-pvr
